JUDGES Q&A CHEAT-SHEET (DEFENSE PREPARATION)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Q1: "Why can't MTN or Telecel just build this themselves?"
Answer:
"Three reasons:
1. Cross-Network Blindness: MTN cannot query Telecel's internal database or AT's 
   SIM-swap records due to commercial competition and regulatory firewalls. MoMo Shield 
   acts as an independent, neutral consortium middleware (similar to GhIPSS for clearing).
2. Agility & Multi-Signal Fusion: Telco core switches are legacy telecommunication 
   infrastructure. They cannot natively run LLM intent classification, behavioral heuristic 
   clustering, and live multi-hop graph analysis in < 120ms without third-party middleware.
3. Universal Protection: We protect the entire financial loop: Network -> Bank -> Switch."

Q2: "What about False Positives? Won't legitimate transfers get blocked?"
Answer:
"We employ a 3-tier risk architecture:
- Low Risk (Score < 40): Allowed silently with zero friction.
- Medium Risk (Score 40 - 74): Soft friction ('warn' / 'delay' 15-minute cooling off). 
  The user is given an interactive choice, preventing false drops on genuine transactions.
- High Risk (Score >= 75): Hard block ('reject') reserved only for confirmed typologies 
  (e.g., active call co-occurrence during payment authorization + brand-new SIM swap). 
In our InnoFemme Benchmark run across 14 scenarios, our False Positive rate is exactly 0%."

Q3: "How does this scale across West Africa?"
Answer:
"The Mobile Money protocol across Francophone and Anglophone Africa (MTN, Orange, 
Moov, Wave) runs on identical GSMA Mobile Money and USSD specifications. Once deployed 
in Ghana, our middleware adapter can plug into Côte d'Ivoire, Senegal, and Nigeria with 
zero re-architecture."
